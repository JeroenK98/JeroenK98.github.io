# Jeroen Kole

PhD candidate in History, University of Antwerp
{: .tagline }

I study how wealth, consumption and domestic life were related in nineteenth-century Antwerp, using probate inventories and inheritance records together with computational methods. My dissertation, *Escaping Inequality? Domesticity in Nineteenth-Century Antwerp*, is supervised by Bruno Blondé, Ilja Van Damme and Wout Saelens.

Alongside the historical work I follow the [MA Digital Text Analysis](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/study/programmes/all-programmes/digital-text-analysis/) and work with Python, R, machine learning and NLP, including stylometry and multilingual text classification.
